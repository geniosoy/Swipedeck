# Swipedeck web editor

Mode: Operate. Audience: someone who just flashed the board and the owner on a daily visit. Job: change what a key shows
and does, organise desktops and pages, set up live desktops and device settings. Constraints: PRODUCT.md. Chosen by the
user on 2026-11-25: the "Studio" direction with its presets docked along the bottom, as in the "Quiet mono" sketch.

Memorable moment: the key you are editing wears one orange, on the board, in the panel and in the desktop list.
Open: nothing blocking.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The board is an object on a quiet table and everything else is within reach of it. It refuses the dark
deck-software arrangement where presets, preview and properties compete as equal boxed panels.

OWN-WORLD: Braun product design: matte light-grey shell (graphite in dark), graphite type in Hanken Grotesk, one signal
orange (#e8641b) for the primary action and the thing being edited, pill switches, hairline rules instead of cards.

STORY: Pick a desktop on the left, see its board in the middle, click a key and edit it on the right, drag a preset up
from the tray below. Save, and the real board changes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 64px top bar (logo, Desktops/Settings, board status; Undo, Redo, Discard changes, Save in orange). A
240px desktop list with a picture of each screen. Centre: page tabs, the board at 2x in a black bezel, columns and rows
under it. Below the board, a preset tray with category tabs, search and at least one full row. Right, 380px: Key /
Desktop switch, the key editor with Look and Action open, Hold and Change look folded. Primary action top right.

FORM: Braun/Dieter Rams product design, candidate 3 of the ordered list; seed:80c67d3d8a6f1133

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
